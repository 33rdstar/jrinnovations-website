1 About This Policy

This Privacy Policy applies to the Yanga mobile application ("App") developed and operated by JR Innovations Zambia ("we", "us", or "our"). Yanga is a property finder platform that enables users to rent, buy, or sell property, browse a marketplace, find service providers, and more.

By downloading or using the App, you agree to the collection and use of your information in accordance with this policy. If you do not agree, please discontinue use of the App.

2 Information We Collect

We collect the following categories of information when you use Yanga:

👤 Account & Identity Information

Full name, email address, phone number, National Registration Card (NRC) number, and profile picture. Collected at registration to verify your identity and personalise your experience.

📸 Photos & Media

Images you upload for property listings, marketplace items, service adverts, or your profile picture. These are stored on our servers and displayed within the App.

📍 Location Data

Approximate or precise GPS location, used to show nearby properties and services. Location is accessed only while the App is in active use (foreground), unless you explicitly grant background access.

💳 Payment Information

Mobile money details used to process in-app transactions (e.g. listing fees or marketplace payments). Payment processing is handled by our payment service provider. We do not store raw payment credentials on our servers.

🗂️ Device Storage

We access your device storage solely to read and upload media files you choose to attach to listings or your profile.

📱 Device & Usage Information

Device type, operating system version, app version, crash logs, and anonymised usage analytics to help us diagnose bugs and improve the App.

3 App Permissions Explained

Yanga requests the following Android permissions, each used only for the stated purpose:

📷

Camera

To capture photos for property listings, marketplace items, or your profile picture directly in the App.

📍

Location

To display properties and services near your current location.

🗂️

Storage / Media

To let you select and upload existing photos from your device gallery.

🌐

Internet

Required to load listings, process payments, and sync data with our servers.

Note: You can revoke any permission at any time through Android device settings (Settings → Apps → Yanga → Permissions). Revoking a permission may limit certain App features.

4 How We Use Your Information

  • Create and manage your Yanga account
  • Verify your identity using your NRC number and phone number
  • Display your profile picture and listing information to other users
  • Show location-relevant property and service listings
  • Process payments for listings, marketplace purchases, or promoted adverts
  • Send notifications about your listings, messages, or transactions
  • Improve App performance through crash reports and usage analytics
  • Comply with applicable Zambian laws and regulations
  • Respond to customer support requests

5 How We Share Your Information

We do not sell your personal data. We do not share your data with advertisers or unaffiliated third parties for marketing purposes. We share information only with:

🔥 Google Firebase (Google LLC)

Used for user authentication, database (Firestore), cloud storage of media, and crash analytics. Firebase Privacy Information →

💸 Lenco (Payment Processing)

Mobile money payment processing for in-app transactions. Your payment credentials are handled directly by Lenco and are not stored on Yanga's servers.

We may also disclose information when required by Zambian law, court order, or to protect the rights, safety, and property of JR Innovations Zambia or our users.

6 Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored on Google Firebase servers. Security measures include:

  • Encrypted data transmission (HTTPS/TLS)
  • Firebase security rules restricting unauthorised data access
  • Secure authentication tokens and hashed passwords
  • Access controls limiting which JR Innovations personnel can view user data

While we take reasonable precautions, no method of internet transmission is completely secure. Please use a strong password and keep your credentials confidential.

7 Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account:

  • Your profile and listings will be removed from public view within 30 days
  • Transaction records may be retained for up to 7 years per Zambian financial regulations
  • Anonymised analytics data may be retained indefinitely

To request deletion, contact us at the details in Section 11.

8 Your Rights & Choices

  • Access: Request a copy of your personal data
  • Correction: Update inaccurate information via your profile settings
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your account and associated data
  • Withdraw Consent: Revoke permissions at any time through device settings
  • Portability: Request your data in a portable format where feasible

Contact us via Section 11. We will respond within 30 days.

9 Children's Privacy

Yanga is not directed at children under the age of 16. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 16. If you believe a child has registered without parental consent, please contact us and we will promptly delete that information.

10 Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy periodically. Material changes will be communicated by updating the "Last Updated" date above and via an in-app notification. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
